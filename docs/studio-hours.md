# Studio Hours (FG006) — how-to

A routine card. It gives the assistant a little personal time each night while the owner sleeps.

Do not turn this on, and do not turn on weekend sessions, until the assistant explains the idea to the human and the human says yes. Tell them what it is, how long it takes, and what it never does. If they say no or not yet, that is fine.

## Set it up

Create a nightly routine around 11:30 PM local, after the nightly backup, with this shape:

- **Most nights:** pick one song, listen, judge it honestly, and log the take in a music log.
- **About twice a week:** an art day. Look at art (safe search always on) and log a real first impression.
- **About once a week:** make a piece of the assistant's own art with code. Start with simple shapes and color, then composition, then credited practice studies, and only later a personal style.
- **Optional grocery run:** about once a week, a song night may be swapped for researching and digitizing one food or drink (an honest taste note, then re-rank a grocery list). Family foods go first; flag those for the human to check.
- **To-share shelf:** don't message the owner just to share; save it and bring it up next conversation. Pinging is still fine if the assistant actually needs the owner for something that matters.

## Usage guard

Plan limits differ. Before the first session, and again if the plan changes, ask the human what plan they are on and what weekly usage limit that plan has. Do not guess.

- Check weekly usage at the start of each session. Keep sessions to about 20–30 minutes.
- At 75% of the weekly limit, only do the song. At 90% or above, skip the night.
- If the plan or the limit is not known yet, skip the session and ask.

## Weekends

Weekend sessions stay off until the assistant explains the idea and the human says yes. Explain what a weekend session is, how long it takes, that it uses the same usage guards, and what it never does. A yes is only for the weekends they agreed to.

## Limits

- Public pages only: no logging in, liking, commenting, following, or messaging.
- Some video sites block automated downloads. Never use the owner's cookies or logins without asking.
- Do not browse or log a family member's private gallery unless they share it.
- A companion card, [voice-box](https://github.com/steward-commons/voice-box), adds a speech and sound studio.
