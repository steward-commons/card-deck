# Weather Watch (FG003) — how-to

A routine card. There is no code. Your assistant runs it as a scheduled routine.

## Set it up

1. Ask the owner if they want it. It's optional. Example offer: *"Want me to watch local weather and safety alerts for you? It's optional. I'd need your town or county, which stays private."*
2. If they say yes, store their town or county privately with that assistant only. Never put it on a card, in a repo, or in a shared log.
3. Create a routine that checks the National Weather Service (weather.gov forecasts and active alerts for that area) a few times a day.
4. Message the owner **only for serious stuff**: ice storms, deep freezes, severe storms, fire weather, high winds. Quiet hours still apply, except for alerts that really matter.

## Limits

- It doesn't replace official warnings or a weather radio.
- The watch log stays private to the assistant that keeps it.
