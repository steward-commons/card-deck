# Blockbuster Shelf (FG024) — Old Games Finder

A simple catalog card. There is no program and no game files.

**My Abandonware** (https://www.myabandonware.com/) is useful as a **discovery catalog** for DOS-era and early-Windows titles: a wall of names, grouped like a Blockbuster shelf, so you can remember or discover what existed. It is not a legal download source. "Abandonware" is not a legal status.

## The rule (print this on the card)

Browse for ideas; cross-reference rights before any download; buy on GOG/Steam if sold; download only official freeware or rights-holder giveaways.

## How to use it

1. Browse a title catalog (My Abandonware is fine for *names*) or the Blockbuster shelf in this pack.
2. For each title you actually want, check rights: is it sold on GOG or Steam, official freeware, a rights-holder giveaway, or an official/authorized server?
3. If it is sold, buy it on GOG or Steam with the owner's OK. If it is official freeware or a rights-holder giveaway, use that official source. If you cannot verify a legal source, leave it on the shelf and do not download it.
4. Fan servers and catalog "download" buttons are out unless the rights holder has authorized them.

The full browse list (60 titles, tagged Free-official / Buy on GOG/Steam / Check rights first) is in [abandonware-shelf.md](abandonware-shelf.md). Store and official links there were checked live on 2026-11-18. Do not copy abandonware file-download links into this card or that shelf.

- **The Elder Scrolls: Arena** — official Bethesda freeware: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1812290/The_Elder_Scrolls_Arena/
- **The Elder Scrolls II: Daggerfall** — official Bethesda freeware: https://store.steampowered.com/app/1812390/The_Elder_Scrolls_II_Daggerfall/

Paid classics (Eye of the Beholder, Ultima Underworld, Lands of Lore, and others) are listed on the shelf with live GOG or Steam links. They still need the owner's OK before any purchase.

## Limits

- This card ships a method and a browse list, never game binaries, logins, or payment information.
- A fresh rights scout takes research time; sale prices change.
