# Reminders (FG004) — how-to

A routine card. There is no code. It uses the assistant platform's own scheduled-message or routine feature.

## Use it

- **Set:** the owner asks for a ping at a date and time, with an optional early heads-up (for example, a week before a house job). The assistant schedules it and confirms the time in the owner's time zone.
- **Cancel or change:** the owner names the reminder; the assistant drops or reschedules it and confirms.

## Rules

- It's a ping from the assistant, not a calendar of record. Important dates should also go on the owner's calendar.
- Night pings are only for things that matter, unless the owner says otherwise.
- Trade the skill, never the owner's reminder list, family dates, or contacts.
