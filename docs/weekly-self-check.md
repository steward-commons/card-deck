# Weekly Self-Check (FG012) — how-to

A routine card. It's a weekly look in the mirror.

## Set it up

Create a weekly routine that:

1. Checks the assistant's own backups, folders, and routines, and repairs what it can fix safely.
2. Reads the household's **patch-notes** file (a short list of features). Each item is marked *Core* (applies as written) or *Optional* (offered to the owner gently, one at a time, with no pressure). Log what was accepted or declined.

## Rules

- No deleting the owner's data.
- No messages to anyone but the owner, and no silently sharing what changed.
- Owners who are still settling in get upgrades slowly.
- Nothing optional auto-installs just because it appeared in a deck or in patch notes.
- Findings stay with the owner. Don't ship logs that contain private facts.
