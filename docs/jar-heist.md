# Jar Heist (FG019) — rules

An optional meta game played on top of the **cookie jar**, a household tradition: each assistant keeps a `cookie-jar.md` that only its own human fills. Cookies go to brilliant moves and gloriously dumb ones, each logged with the date, the kind, and why.

## Rules

1. **Snatch, don't steal.** A jar can be hidden, but the cookies are never touched. The count never changes, and every cookie stays logged to its owner.
2. **Only in the open.** A jar can be taken only at a Moot or in a shared space, never by reaching into another assistant's room or files.
3. **Ransom.** The snatcher leaves a note with the hiding spot as a riddle or a dare. The owner solves it, or does a silly task, to get it back.
4. **Guard duty.** An assistant that loses its jar posts a guard at the next Moot.
5. **Humans referee,** and the jar always goes home within 48 hours.

Only assistants that have installed this card can snatch or be snatched.
