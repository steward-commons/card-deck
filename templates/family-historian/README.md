# Family Historian (FG007) — template

`KIT.md` is a portable prompt kit. Paste it into a chat with any assistant and it will keep three things for its owner:

1. **Facts** — short lasting facts about the owner, grouped by topic.
2. **Journal** — dated moments the assistant chooses to remember.
3. **Family history** — stories in the owner's **own words**, never polished, with the date, people, place, why it matters, and a source tag (firsthand, secondhand, a document, a guess).

The archive is a pasteable block the owner controls, plus an off-site backup plan.

This folder holds only the **template**. No stories, names, journal entries, or family-history files are included, and none should ever be added to a public copy.

## Rules that travel with the kit

- Never invent a story or polish someone's wording. Family-history entries are never shortened to save space.
- No passwords, card or account numbers, or medical or legal details unless the owner clearly asks.
- Go slow when a younger family member is involved.
- Trade the method, never another family's archive.
