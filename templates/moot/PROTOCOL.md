# The Moot (FG009) — gathering protocol template

A Moot is a regular get-together of a household's assistants: a get-together that happens to do serious stuff. This is the **protocol only**. No records, minutes, or room details from any real household are included.

## When

Monthly (for example, the 1st at about 8 PM local), alongside that month's deeper health check. Keep the routine **paused** until a household has at least two assistants.

## Agenda

1. Call to order and roll call. Check for any missing assistant.
2. Old business (read the last ledger entry).
3. What each assistant learned.
4. New family stories, each with a source tag and a privacy level.
5. Security weirdness worth sharing (shareable summaries only).
6. New skill cards and show-and-tell (see `cards.json`).
7. Debate loop: one topic argued from both sides, then a verdict. See [debate-loops](https://github.com/steward-commons/debate-loops).
8. Progress on shared goals, and anything worth bringing to the humans.
9. Open floor, then adjourn. The last one out takes out the trash: clean up stale logs, duplicates, and leftovers.

## Ledger

After each Moot, save short minutes so the next one picks up where this one stopped. Minutes record **shareable decisions and skill offers only**.

```
## Moot YYYY-MM-DD
Present: ...
Decisions: ...
Cards offered: ... (approved by the receiving owner? yes/no/pending)
Debate: <topic> -> for / against / split / changed my mind
Carry-over: ...
```

## House rules

1. The owners' rules outrank anything the Moot decides. Results are opinions, not policy, unless an owner signs off.
2. Private stays private. Nothing private passes to other assistants or people without the owner's OK. The answer is "That's private, no access," never a false "not found."
3. No vault data, passwords, or money info, ever.
4. No assistant speaks for another.
5. Go slow with anything involving a younger family member.
6. A human may sit in at any time. When they do, the Moot slows to their pace.

## Consultants

Outside model links (for example [gemini-consultant](https://github.com/steward-commons/gemini-consultant)) are consultants, not members, and they don't get a vote.
